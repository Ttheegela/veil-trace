## Q&A prep (short, honest answers)

**1. How do you know the routes are new?**
Two independent sources. Shipment records show Kyrgyzstan, UAE and Kazakhstan chip routes starting April to May 2022. Those records mostly start in 2022, so we checked countries' own export reports to the UN: Kyrgyzstan near zero in 2019 to 2021, then $0.66M in 2022 and $13.5M in 2023; Kazakhstan about $0.25M, then $18.3M in 2022; Armenia near zero, then $13.1M. Declared values, not proof of every shipment.

**2. Isn't naming companies risky?**
Yes, so we only name companies already on an official list, and we checked each listing on an official page, not a sponsor flag. Unlisted firms stay unnamed and unshaded. We never name individuals. Everything is a lead, not a finding.

**3. What can't your data see?**
Countries with no trade records of their own in our main source, like Kyrgyzstan, Armenia and Hong Kong; most chip records into Russia come from Russia's own customs data, which thins after late 2023. No ship positions. Iran's oil relabelling doesn't show up at all. And we can't prove any chip reached a weapon.

**4. How would a compliance officer use this?**
Screen the product, the buyer and the route, not just the seller's name. If a known listed buyer is receiving the same part from a seller that appeared last month, that is a question to ask before shipping. Example of why: a firm with the same tax number, registration number and address as a UK-listed company still shows as clean in a sponsor tool.

**5. What did Microsoft / Fabric add?**

**6. What's next?**
Watch patterns, not just names: in a back-test, 240 pairs of Russia-programme firms at the same address were listed in different waves, a median of about 167 days apart. Add sources for the dark countries. Keep the skeptic step before anything is published. Agency-only leads go to agencies, never to a slide.

**7. Does a listing stop the trade?**
Not always. The buyer from the "new names" screen, Testkomplekt, kept receiving shipments at least 22 months after its own US listing (drawer: The lag, More in the data). And RM Design and Development left the Kyrgyz route 24 days before its listing, then has 40 records after the listing under its own name, declared as leaving Turkey. Declared countries, not physical routes; counts after a listing are minimums.
