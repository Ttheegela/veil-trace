# Open leads and next tests

These are **leads for review, not findings**. Companies that are not on an official US, UK, EU or UN list
are described, not named; the names stay with the team and are shared only with an agency on request.
Innocent explanations are listed for each one because they are often the likeliest reading.

Each lead says what we saw, why it might matter, and the next test that would confirm or kill it.

## A. Who kept supplying the listed buyers

1. **An unlisted Kyrgyz trading firm stepped in after RM Design's listing.** After RM Design and Development
   was listed (OFAC 2023-07-20), an unlisted Kyrgyz firm sent 8 records to its buyer Basis Trade Prosoft (OFAC
   2023-07-20): 3 from Turkey and 5 from the UAE, Sept to Dec 2023. A UAE firm with a similar name sent 31
   records on one day; whether they are the same firm is not verified.
   - Innocent: normal re-sourcing by the buyer; firms sharing common trading names.
   - Next test: registry IDs and addresses for both firms; are they related to RM Design's owners or address?
2. **Other suppliers to RM Design's buyers after its listing.** Emsystech (India; on the OFAC list, date to
   confirm, our list file suggests 2024-10-30) sent 53 records to Basis Trade Prosoft, Sept to Nov 2023. Two
   unlisted Chinese suppliers sent 30 and 10 records to Region-Prof (OFAC 2023-07-20). First page of results
   only, so these are minimums.
   - Next test: confirm Emsystech's date on the official OFAC page; pull full pages.
3. **An unlisted distributor and an unlisted Shenzhen trader kept supplying Testkomplekt** after the six listed
   suppliers stopped; one sent its last record 8 days after Testkomplekt's US listing.
   - Innocent: licensed or uncontrolled goods.
   - Next test: product codes against the Common High Priority List (CHPL); end-use statements.
4. **A "second ring" in Tradeverifyd.** An unlisted Russian buyer is linked to 5 listed suppliers (2 on the US
   asset-freeze list, 1 by name only, 2 on the BIS Entity List); an unlisted Hong Kong freight forwarder
   appears on 74 of its 76 supplier links. Links carry no dates or volumes.
   - Innocent: forwarders carry goods for anyone; the buyer may itself consolidate cargo.
   - Next test: find these links in dated shipment records; check whether they predate the listings.
5. **An unlisted Indian seller switched buyers** from Tornetcom (OFAC 2024-10-30) to a new Moscow buyer right
   after Tornetcom's listing. Counts not yet verified.

## B. Same company or not?

6. **A Turkish-registered record carrying the exact OFAC alias of RM Design and Development** (1,883 records,
   2022-04-21 to 2023-07-12; buyers include flagged Russian firms). It shares the name but has a Turkish company
   form and sits in a 194-tenant tower. Identity not verified.
   - Next test: Turkish trade registry number and officers; compare with the OFAC entry's identifiers.
7. **GQ Solution Elektronik** (Istanbul; on the OFAC list, list-file date about 2024-05-01) sits at the exact
   Istanbul unit RM Design used. A law-firm alert reports a US forfeiture case over aircraft landing gear bought
   for RM Design; the Justice Department page would not load.
   - Next test: read the court filing or the Justice Department release.
8. **Two unlisted Russian firms share a room and an address with listed STRELOI EKOMMERTS** and a supplier with
   it; one was registered 2022-10-27. The address has 87 tenants, so this is weak to medium.
   - Next test: tax numbers, directors (from official registries, not published), and dated shipments.
9. **A Tradeverifyd buyer "llc intellect telecom"** may be Intellekt Telekom (OFAC 2024-10-30); no ID on the
   link record. Our checker missed the spelling.
10. **The sponsor's EU flags on two large Russian telecom buyers** (the largest Turkey-route buyer and the second
    largest top-10 buyer) do not match the EU, US or UK asset-freeze lists we hold.
    - Next test: check EU Regulation 833/2014 annexes (export restrictions), not only the asset-freeze list.

## D. Climate commodities

17. **An unlisted Kazakh firm near the Russian border**, registered 2022-07-20 as a fertiliser wholesaler, sent
    18 records of birch plywood in the Russian 1525 by 1525 mm format, declared Kazakh-made, to EU buyers from
    2023-08-29 to 2026-06-18; at least 12 are after the EU's 2024-05-14 duty extension.
    - Innocent: new Kazakh plywood plants, a default origin field, pre-ban stock.
    - Next test: EU anti-fraud office (OLAF) or customs checks; mill marks; the firm's own production capacity.
18. **Russian plywood brand rows leaving Turkey:** 17 records name Russia's big plywood maker; none go to the EU
    or US. Low priority.
19. **Russian aluminium in Turkish wheels.** Whether wheels cast in Turkey from Russian metal fall under the
    US origin rule (2024-04-13) or EU rules: not verified. Turkish import records of Russian metal stop on
    2024-07-24, while Russian export records continue to 2025-03-26 with some rows naming no destination.
20. **Malaysia's bearing exports to Russia** jumped ($59.7M to $126.5M, 2021 to 2023) then fell to $5.7M in
    2025. Unchecked.

## E. Payments and AI chips

21. **Two Dubai gold traders** were the top UAE buyers of Russian gold records; neither is on a list we checked.
22. **A Malaysian data-hosting firm** registered 2024-02-07 at Johor data-centre addresses received 2,033
    shipments from 2025-01-02 to 2026-02-26 (USA 802, China 638, Hong Kong 350), mostly network gear and server
    parts. Press reports link a firm of the same name to a Singapore company under police investigation; the
    parent link is not verified, and the maker says it found no diversion.
    - Innocent: Southeast Asia is building real data centres.
23. **Remote rental of AI chips** is legal under current rules; a House bill on remote access was reported in
    January 2026 but not verified on an official page.
